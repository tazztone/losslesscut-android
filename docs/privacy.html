Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 2026-08-15

This privacy policy applies to the LosslessCut Android app (hereby referred to as "Application") created by Tazztone as an Open Source app. This service is provided by Tazztone at no cost and is intended for use as is.

Data Collection and Use

We do not collect any personal data or usage data.

The Application processes all video files locally on your device. No video content, metadata, or other personal information is transmitted to any external server.

Permissions

The Application does not request broad storage or network permissions. When you choose media, Android grants access to the selected content through the system picker. Exports are written through MediaStore or a folder you select with the Storage Access Framework.

  • Selected media access: Required to read the files you choose to edit and to write exports to a destination you choose.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

I may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. I will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
